FMC NDA

This Agreement is made by the parties identified below in connection with the business meeting described here. It applies to information shared before, during, or after that meeting in connection with its purpose.

Parties

1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means all non-public information relating to FMC or its actual or proposed activities, whether disclosed orally, visually, in writing, electronically, by demonstration, or otherwise, and whether or not marked confidential. It includes everything said, shown, presented, or discussed at or in connection with the meeting; business plans, strategy, products, services, pricing, finances, forecasts, methods, processes, software, designs, research, security information, suppliers, customers, personnel, and the identity or terms of any business opportunity. The fact that discussions are taking place and their status are also confidential.

2. Purpose and non-disclosure

The recipient will use Confidential Information only to consider or pursue the meeting purpose stated above, and for no other purpose. The recipient must keep it strictly confidential and must not publish, announce, disclose, transmit, or make it available to any other person without FMC’s prior written consent. The recipient must not photograph, record, livestream, or make copies of meeting materials except as FMC expressly authorizes in writing.

3. Limited access and protection

Access is permitted only to the recipient’s representatives who genuinely need the information for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these terms. The recipient is responsible for their compliance. The recipient will protect the information with at least reasonable care, promptly notify FMC of any suspected loss or unauthorized access or disclosure, and reasonably assist in limiting its effects.

4. No rights granted

All Confidential Information remains FMC’s property. No license, ownership, or other rights are granted by disclosure. Neither party is required to proceed with a transaction or other relationship.

5. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information the recipient can demonstrate through written records: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the recipient without a confidentiality duty before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality duty; or (d) is independently developed without using or referring to Confidential Information.

6. Required disclosure

If disclosure is required by law or a valid legal order, the recipient will, where legally permitted, give FMC prompt written notice and reasonably cooperate with efforts to seek confidential treatment. The recipient will disclose only the portion legally required.

7. Return or deletion

Upon FMC’s written request, the recipient will promptly return or permanently delete Confidential Information and copies under its control, and confirm completion on request. A copy may be retained only to the extent required by law or routine, inaccessible backup systems, and remains subject to this Agreement.

8. Duration

This Agreement applies to disclosures made in connection with the meeting. The recipient’s obligations continue for three (3) years from each disclosure. Information that qualifies as a trade secret remains protected for as long as it retains that status under applicable law.

9. General terms

This Agreement is the entire agreement about its subject and may be changed only in a writing signed by both parties. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply. The laws of govern this Agreement, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted to the extent allowed by applicable law.
