This agreement establishes the terms under which confidential project, business, technical, and operational information may be shared between the parties.

Confidentiality notice
All materials marked or reasonably understood as confidential must be protected from unauthorized use, disclosure, or distribution.

1. Definition of confidential information

Confidential information includes project plans, source material, designs, credentials, financial information, research, customer information, and any non-public information disclosed in writing, visually, electronically, or verbally.

2. Permitted use

The receiving party may use confidential information only to evaluate, build, operate, or support the agreed project. Access is limited to people who need the information and are bound by equivalent confidentiality duties.

3. Protection and return

  1. Use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.
  2. Do not copy or disclose information outside the agreed project scope.
  3. Return or securely delete confidential material when requested or when the project ends.

4. Exclusions and term

Information already public, independently developed, lawfully received from another source, or required to be disclosed by law is excluded. These obligations remain in force for three years after the last disclosure.
